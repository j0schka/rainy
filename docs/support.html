FAQ

Where does the weather data come from?
In Germany and neighboring countries, the app uses precipitation radar from the German Weather Service (DWD) via Bright Sky, with 1 km resolution and 5-minute steps. Everywhere else it uses the Open-Meteo 15-minute forecast, and the radar map comes from RainViewer.
The app shows “?” instead of a number.
The app needs your location to look up rain nearby. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Time To Rain and allow “While Using the App”. Also check your internet connection.
I don’t get rain alerts.
Allow notifications in Settings › Notifications › Time To Rain. Alerts are sent about 15 minutes before rain is expected, and only while the app has fetched a forecast recently.
How accurate is the countdown?
About ±5 minutes with DWD radar data. Elsewhere it is based on a 15-minute forecast, so expect roughly ±15 minutes.
Does the app collect my data?
No. There is no account, no tracking and no ads. See the privacy policy.
